Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we collect the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide you with the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and enabling filters. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app for your personal use.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical precautions to protect it from unauthorized access. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data brokers. Any external services used to operate the platform (such as hosting providers) are bound by confidentiality obligations and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to request deletion of your data, please reach out to us. We will respond to all privacy-related requests within a reasonable timeframe.
